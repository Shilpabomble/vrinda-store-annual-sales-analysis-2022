📖 Project Overview

Vrinda Store is an online fashion retailer selling through multiple e-commerce channels (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Meesho, Ajio, Nalli, etc.). This project analyses the store's 2022 sales data and presents the results in a single interactive Excel dashboard built with Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers and data cleaning techniques.

---

❓ Business Problem

Vrinda Store has a lot of transaction data but no clear view of who its customers are, where they live, how they buy, and which channels perform best. Without this understanding, marketing spend and sales strategy are based on guesswork.

---

🎯 Objectives

Create an annual sales report for 2022.

Understand the customer profile (gender, age group, location).

Identify the best-performing channels, states, categories and months.

Provide data-driven recommendations to increase sales in 2023.

---

💡 Why I Built This Project

To practise the complete data analysis workflow: raw data → cleaning → analysis → visualization → business insight.

To solve a real-world business problem rather than just make charts.

To build a portfolio project that shows I can convert data into decisions.

To strengthen my skills in Excel (Pivot Tables, Charts, Slicers, Dashboards), which is the foundation of every data analyst role.

🔄 Approach / Methodology

Understand the objective – defined the business questions first.

Data cleaning – removed duplicates and nulls, corrected formats, created helper columns (e.g., Age Group: Teenager / Adult / Senior; Month).

Data analysis – built Pivot Tables to answer each business question.

Visualization – converted each Pivot Table into a suitable chart.

Dashboard – combined all charts on one sheet and connected Slicers to all of them.

Insights & recommendations – interpreted the results into business actions.

🔍 Key Insights

👩 Women are the main buyers – about 64–65% of sales come from women vs ~36% from men.

📍 Maharashtra, Karnataka and Uttar Pradesh are the top 3 states by sales (≈ ₹2.99M, ₹2.65M and ₹2.10M), followed by Telangana and Tamil Nadu.

🎂 Adults (30–49 yrs) are the biggest contributing age group at about 50% of orders.

🛒 Amazon, Flipkart and Myntra together contribute roughly 80% of orders, with Amazon alone at ~35%.

✅ About 92% of orders are delivered, while cancellations, returns and refunds are small (~8% combined).

📉 Sales and order volume are highest in the early part of the year and show a declining trend through the later months, which signals an opportunity for seasonal campaigns.

---

✅ Business Recommendation

> **Target women customers aged 30–49 living in Maharashtra, Karnataka and Uttar Pradesh by running ads, offers and coupons on Amazon, Flipkart and Myntra.**

Additional suggestions:

Run festive/seasonal offers in the low-sales months to reverse the declining trend.

Investigate reasons for cancellations and returns to push delivery rate above 92%.

Expand marketing in Telangana and Tamil Nadu, which are close behind the top 3 states.
